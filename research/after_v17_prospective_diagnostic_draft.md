## Fundamental uncertainty and competing explanations

Retention improves the specified mechanism yet most goal windows fail. Does the
learned relation already miss the immediate consequences at the histories actually
visited, and does its action-dependent component describe alternative commands?
Ordinary forecast MSE mixes drift, action response, noise and ambiguous history.
An accurate conditional mean can also be inaccurate for a particular hidden
world. Neither a new architecture nor a local-prefix estimator is assumed needed.

First evaluate every saved issued-action prediction without additional physics.
Only then evaluate a fixed, outcome-independent subset of saved prefixes with
external action forks. No diagnostic value may select a replacement model or
setting in the original study. A subsequent learner comparison requires its own
prospective protocol and fresh evaluation. The independent prospective critique
is cartesian_stream_v17_prefix_diagnostic_independent.md.

## Interpretation and audit requirements

A fork provides one world's potential consequence F(X,u). It does not provide
the learner's conditional mean E[F(X,u)|H]. In expected squared contrast error,
remaining conditional world variance adds to conditional-mean model error. Thus
large per-world contrast errors cannot alone distinguish hidden ambiguity,
insufficient history/metric, unsupported contexts, fitting bias or uncertainty.
Near neighbors are not support certificates; close but physically different
records do not prove the full ordinary prefix lacks the relevant cue.

Accurate local consequences with failed goals would shift attention toward long
horizons, decisions and uncertainty. Poor immediate forecasts leave history,
drift and support live. Accurate zero-command drift with poor contrasts would
focus the acquired action relationship, without proving an architecture needed.
These are discriminating possibilities, not claims of unique causal diagnosis.
A shadow matched-prefix fit or a new prefix learner is optional future work,
requiring a new design; neither is silently added to this measurement.

Require independent NumPy/affine reconstruction of all Stage A/B forecasts,
statistics/noise/variance identities and data flow, plus independent Hamiltonian
replay of all Stage B intervals and fixed Radau checks before interpreting fork
numbers. Preserve any numerical failures before repair. No modern superiority,
new principle, conditional identifiability or reliable control follows solely
from a diagnostic. Use the existing owner PC/.venv, no new packages/services,
no canonical overwrites, and publish verified progress over the existing SSH
