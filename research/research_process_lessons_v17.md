# Lessons and course correction after v17

2026-10-20. Process correction authorized by the user. This is not a scientific
release or a new experimental result. Original instructions and historical
handoffs remain available in Git at b46226f200167b42868bfbfac40bdd92438abf2c.
The scientific ledgers and released evidence are unchanged.

## Honest assessment

Five hours of work through v17 did not deliver a defensible new general learning
framework or competitive result. Valid mechanics, conventional constructions and
negative results were produced. Their scientific scope does not justify treating
the overall allocation of time as effective progress toward the user's objective.

The decisive recent result is negative: fresh retained local learning passes
55/192 sustained windows, frozen acquired-library control 19/192 and the
privileged reference 192/192. Retention improves relative cost without reliability.
The pilot's 22/24 did not extrapolate. Neither verifying those numbers nor fixing
an auditor's roundoff criterion creates a new learning mechanism.

## Why the process failed

The coordinator selected locally tractable follow-ups without comparing their
combined cost with building a consequential mechanism. A theorem suggested a
diagnostic, a diagnostic suggested an ablation, and a numerical discrepancy
suggested more replay. Each step could be defended individually while the
program's purpose receded.

Verification often preceded a decision about scientific value. Large amounts of
independent fitting/physics reconstruction, provenance tooling and reporting
increased confidence in narrow statements. The opportunity cost was substantial.
The checks were not all unnecessary: some caught real errors. Their scope and
sequencing were disproportionate to the capability demonstrated.

Repeated publication and instructions to continue immediately made it too easy
to follow the current branch. Broad permission for theoretical detours lacked a
bounded construction or consequential-impossibility endpoint. Independent agents
were used heavily to check correctness and too weakly to challenge whether the
branch deserved another hour. Supplied features, family, metric and planner could
produce apparent capability while too little scrutiny reached what was learned.
These are coordinator judgment failures, not proof that rigor or first-principles
research is unproductive.

## Concrete changes

AGENTS.md now requires a consequence gate, a concrete learning-operation proposal,
a supplied-versus-acquired account and a serious competitive comparison plan.
Construction and discriminating evaluation are the main work, with default
30-minute/90-minute/two-hour decision deadlines for long campaigns and hourly
reassessment thereafter. Exceptions require a consequential reason and bounded
endpoint, not automatic renewal. These limits control effort; they do not demand
a forced breakthrough or an untested implementation.

Verification is staged: cheap critical checks, scientific screening, then
independent auditing of the consequential claim. Expand only when sensitivity,
errors or claim scope warrant it. Reuse validated components. An unresolved
correctness issue cannot be ignored to meet a time quota; narrow the claim or
stop the branch instead.

A failed method does not automatically earn an exhaustive diagnostic program.
Require a diagnostic to change the next mechanism decision, start with the
cheapest adequate check and limit repeated rescues. Independent direction
criticism must address opportunity cost, supplied intelligence and baseline
strength, not just algebra. Publishing is durable memory, not a progress metric.

The operational goal is saved in research/campaign_goal.md. Older handoff plans
for all-record prequential analysis and fixed fork banks are preserved as
unexecuted proposals, demoted from the default next action. The next campaign
must choose its route afresh. Do not replace one rigid agenda with a prescribed
prefix estimator, world model, neural family or other architecture.

## What must not change

Keep scientific honesty, first-principles freedom, serious prior-art reading,
independent falsification, fair comparisons and complete historical corrections.
Do not confuse speed with progress or cut necessary correctness checks merely
because previous checks consumed too much time. Do not relabel known work as a
new framework, cherry-pick transfer cases, weaken competitors, or claim novelty
from independent derivation. If modern methods remain stronger, report it.

Trust must be rebuilt through visible changes in code, acquired capability and
fair results. More promises, instruction documents and ledger versions cannot
supply that evidence. This revision is complete when saved and reviewed; the
next user-authorized campaign must demonstrate whether it works.
