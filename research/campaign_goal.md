# Revised campaign goal: construct a consequential learning mechanism

2026-10-20. User-authorized course correction after v17. This is the operational
objective for the next campaign, governed by AGENTS.md. Research remains PAUSED
until the user resumes it. This file is not a claim that the app's stored Goal
text has been replaced, and it does not launch a new campaign.

## Ultimate objective

Discover from first principles, implement and rigorously evaluate our own learning
mechanism, with the ambition of competing with strong modern methods and scaling
toward unfamiliar nonlinear physical systems, robotics and articulated bodies.
Mechanistic understanding and capability matter together. Do not substitute a
new name, simulator, feature menu or numerical solver for a new learning operation.
Do not promise originality or competitive success before evidence exists.

## Next campaign objective

Identify a consequential limitation in acquiring or using transferable knowledge
from experience. Derive a concrete candidate learning rule from explicit
assumptions, construct it, and test whether it addresses that limitation against
competent conventional and relevant modern alternatives. The candidate must make
clear what the programmer supplies, what changes through experience, and why that
change should support a new condition or useful decision.

The route is open. Do not preselect a state, model, representation, reward,
network, memory scheme or other architecture. Existing methods are serious
explanations and competitors. Independent rediscovery is acceptable, but it must
be identified; an own-framework claim needs substantive mechanistic or practical
content beyond restating a known algorithm. If a rediscovery has no substantive
advantage, retire it. Legitimate labels, task inputs and disclosed shared priors
remain permissible; do not hand-code the capability claimed as acquired.

## Required campaign outputs

- An executable candidate learning mechanism with explicit inputs, update and use
  of experience, plus a first-principles explanation and falsifier.
- A supplied-versus-acquired account and a meaningful test of what learning adds.
- A discriminating comparison against a competent simple method and a strong
  relevant modern method for capability claims, with honest data, compute,
  tuning and information access. Initial screening can be smaller; it cannot
  establish competitive performance by itself.
- A declared held-out transfer condition that tests the claimed reusable knowledge,
  beyond new noise seeds or goal repetitions on the same supplied body.
- Reproducible actual results, failed cases, independent criticism of consequence
  and fairness, and verification proportional to the claim.
- A clear decision: continue because a specific advantage survives, revise because
  a discriminating observation warrants it, or reject the branch. No manufactured
  success or compulsory new ledger.

These are outputs to pursue, not permission to fabricate them. If the candidate
cannot be built or loses, record the obstacle or defeat and its consequences.
A consequential impossibility result may replace a construction outcome only
when it actually changes the design space; a narrow technical theorem does not.

## Execution boundaries

For a campaign lasting hours: a focused plan within 30 minutes, executable
candidate or demonstrated obstacle within 90 minutes, first discriminating
comparison by two hours where feasible, then an hourly direction decision. Budget
necessary long training prospectively. Do not spend all allotted time on simple
questions, audit machinery or increasingly detailed explanations of an old toy.
Use existing validated tools and physics. After two serious unsuccessful
formulations without a justified next mechanism, end the attempt with an honest
handoff rather than drift. Routine checkpoints do not require user approval.

The unexecuted post-v17 diagnostics are optional evidence tools. They are NOT the
next campaign goal, not a prerequisite to construction, and not an entitlement to
replay every record or run thousands of forks. Use one only if a bounded result
will choose between materially different learning mechanisms or experiments.

## Success and honesty

Early success is a candidate advantage that survives a meaningful, fair falsifier
and justifies stronger testing. A substantive competitive claim requires strong
relevant baselines and generalization evidence under declared resources. A new
framework or breakthrough requires considerably more than one toy result.

Negative evidence can complete a bounded attempt without achieving the ultimate
objective. The research remains unsuccessful at its ultimate objective until
credible evidence supports an actual advance. Preserve the complete v17 and
prior ledgers; continue from their evidence without inheriting their agenda.
