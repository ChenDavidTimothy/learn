## First-principles construction question

A candidate route concerns experience-acquired applicability: when can prior
behavior be substituted into a new objective/constraint combination without
breaking other requirements? This must concern actual acquired capability,
not new orders of supplied joint goals or new masses in a hand-specified family.
Persistent experience must reduce the cost of a genuinely unseen combination;
removing the relevant experience must remove the claimed benefit. Do not supply
the skill labels, preservation predicates, decomposition or oracle actions that
constitute the claimed discovery.

An elementary diagnostic (not a novel theorem or experiment): start observable
bits A=B=0. Isolated trials a->10 and b->01 fit both a world where actions set one
bit while preserving the other and a world where they reset the other. The
sequence ab achieves 11 only in the first. A b-after-a trial distinguishes them.
Endpoint achievements alone cannot justify compositional reuse. Under full
observable Markov transition knowledge, ordinary planning already handles this;
the counterexample establishes no new algorithm or gap against that explanation.

The independent theory auditor derived contextual program substitution as an
alternative to endpoint-skill membership. Its missing operation is acquiring and
transferring a compact preservation/applicability relation from ordinary
experience with less interaction/computation than competent learned transition
planning. A generic counterexample-guided rewrite loop is already known. No
specific new operation has yet survived this comparison. The next bounded pass

## Challenge the common framing

All three attempts still judged reusable knowledge chiefly through short-term
consequence prediction from supplied q/v/action features. A different scientific
question is now under review: **can experience acquire reusable control knowledge
that reliably composes for objectives specified after acquisition?**

The relevant distinction is reliable closed-loop decisions, not merely replacing
one-step prediction with a longer prediction horizon. An experienced segment is
not automatically a feedback skill. On an inertial body, identical positions
with different velocities support different continuations. Any proposed reuse
operation must preserve the arrival information relevant to its next decision.
These are design constraints, not new discoveries or evidence that existing RL
cannot handle the problem. No particular state ontology, representation or policy
architecture is required by that reasoning.

A meaningful later test would measure completion, settling, interaction/compute
cost and retention on fresh goal combinations, without resets between subgoals.
Both candidate and competitors must receive the SAME complete objective and
ordinary information. A new composition mechanism must not receive future goals
that are hidden from a goal-conditioned competitor. Limited random action coverage
must not be mislabeled a representation failure; equal acquisition or competent
offline treatment is necessary. These conditions govern a future proposal;
no new dataset, control run or benchmark is selected here.

## Existing explanations that the next proposal must confront

[HER](https://papers.nips.cc/paper/7090-hindsight-experience-replay.pdf) already
reuses achieved outcomes through goal relabeling and off-policy learning. Its
method was checked by the independent critic. Generic hindsight reuse is therefore
not a new operation, and an old implementation alone is not the complete modern
comparison.

[Forward–backward representations](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2021/file/003dd617c12d444ff9c80f717c3fa982-Paper.pdf):
read the representation construction and policy/reward mapping, with the critic
checking Sections 3–4. FB already learns long-run occupancy from reward-free
experience for rewards specified later. Its assumptions and approximation quality
matter; its existence does not establish transfer to changed dynamics or arbitrary
history-dependent objectives without additional machinery.

[2026 FB analysis](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.11399v2): examined Sections 3.3–4.1.
It questions classical fixed-point justification for the coupled practical update
and proposes fixed-behavior-policy representation fitting with policy improvement.
This is an existing substantive alternative, not our derivation or a proof that
all practical FB variants diverge. A future competitor choice must account for
such developments rather than use only original FB as a straw target.

[SoRB](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/file/5c48ff18e0a47baaf81d8b8ea51eec92-Paper.pdf)
already constructs replay-buffer planning using learned goal reachability. Generic
trajectory stitching and graph search are not sufficient successor proposals.
USHER's stochastic hindsight-bias correction was also located at its primary
PMLR page, but its full method was not evaluated; do not call that issue novel.

## First-principles reframing

The two screens supplied the experiences, the grouping of experiences by body,
and a batch of related training bodies. They tested how experience conditions a
prediction rule. A deployed learner additionally has to decide what experience
to obtain, which past knowledge is applicable, and what reusable knowledge to
change after an encounter. Those are separate choices; a new feature head does
not automatically address them. Neither screen demonstrates a fundamental
failure of established approaches at those broader choices.

Two tempting next steps are therefore insufficient without a sharper derivation:
"choose informative actions" and "keep learning between tasks." These identify
capabilities, not yet a mechanism or an established gap. More information about
an entire model need not change a useful decision, and retaining data need not
provide beneficial transfer. These are starting distinctions, not new theorems.

## Serious existing accounts checked before another implementation

[FLEX, Blanke and Lelarge, ICML 2023](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/blanke23a/blanke23a.pdf):
read sections 3.3–5, especially the one-step information objective and nonlinear
parameter linearization. It already selects actions through D-optimal design,
with a computational approximation for nonlinear models and online model updates.
Fisher/log-determinant exploration around a learned model is consequently a
serious existing baseline, not a sufficient new proposal. The local approximation
and information objective have stated scope; their existence does not establish
decision-optimal exploration or settle open-ended learning.

[OMLA, Zhu et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2503.18684): read method IV and
experimental setup V-A, rather than infer the mechanism from its title. It uses
task-specific LoRA adapters, online meta-learning from retained task datasets,
then fine-tuning on a new task. Its setting supplies task descriptions and
demonstration datasets. Accumulating data and learning adaptation priors online
are already addressed; attaching either to our current predictor would need
an independently derived distinction and a consequential test. This is not a
head-to-head reproduction or a judgment of all continual learning.

Other search hits, including OCCAM and 2026 papers, were located but their actual
methods were not yet read. Do not cite them as evaluated competitors or infer
state-of-the-art performance from abstracts. ALPaCA/CoDA were strong executable
competitors for the completed screens, not proof that the full modern field was
covered.
