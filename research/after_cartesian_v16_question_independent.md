# Independent prospective question after Cartesian reuse

2026-10-20. Written while the frozen eight-corpus Cartesian study is running. This note proposes a scientific question, not a selected implementation, completed result, required release, or instruction to add more methods to that study. The existing common-direction and isotropic-prior comparison should finish unchanged and be interpreted under its own contract.

## What the present question can finish

Ordinary Bayesian conditioning and decision theory with the correct joint law already answer the current inference/decision problem. There is no need to invent a competing principle merely because the answer is established. Learning an ordinary useful prior from experience, then making better decisions through it, is a legitimate construction. The mixture/Gaussian distinction is a question about approximation and distributional shape, not evidence that one universal architecture has been derived.

The experimenter presently supplies more than an inference rule: exact Cartesian preparation/rest, a local linear torque-response vocabulary, its double-integrator propagation, coefficient persistence, and the grouping under which responses belong together. The learner acquires values and variation inside that language. It does not acquire the conditional relationship between motion, configuration, action and consequence in general. This boundary should be accepted plainly, rather than repaired by calling increasingly elaborate parameter learning discovery of dynamics.

The next consequential uncertainty is therefore not another covariance identity or a finer exploration optimizer. It is whether useful relationships among experiences can be acquired and reused when their validity is no longer certified by the preparation and fixed response law.

## Candidate first-principles question

**When can one experience legitimately substitute for another in deciding what to do, and can the system acquire and revise that interchangeability from observed consequences?**

This framing does not require explicit physical states, world models, latent classes, rewards, or named concepts. They are possible ways of implementing the answer. A library of episodes, a causal history-to-consequence relation, a direct history/goal-to-action mechanism, or a conventional inferred physical description could each preserve the same useful distinctions. The scientific claim concerns acquired transferable relationships, not the internal noun chosen to describe them.

Two histories need not predict every conceivable future identically to support the same decision. Conversely, a small one-step mean error does not ensure that substituting one history for another preserves a feasible stopping action. Define the intended family of decisions and observation/intervention opportunities first. Then ask which differences in experience actually change useful choices. This avoids demanding either a complete predictive description or one fixed representation before the problem is known.

## Why unknown motion alone is not the next result

In the exact constant-response surrogate, one first endpoint can confound initial motion and response:

    p1 = p0 + T*v0 + .5*T^2*B*u0.

More appropriately spaced observations and nondegenerate actions can separate unknown motion from coefficients. Deriving that rank condition is valuable for preventing an unfair comparison. It still assumes the relationship to be learned, and does not explain how a learner acquires that relationship from experience. It is a diagnostic for an implementation, not by itself the scientific next step.

The physical issue is wider: the same observed discrepancy can reflect motion that was not summarized, a response changing with configuration, noise, or an inadequate reused relation. A system need not name those explanations, but it must avoid treating all inconsistent consequences as evidence for the same fixed coefficient. A detector or an explicit model-revision module is only one possible response; ordinary ongoing conditional learning may suffice.

## What would have to emerge from experience

A useful next investigation should remove at least one *structural certification*, not merely add a more difficult target. Candidate missing relations include:

- Which observed context and recent experience change the consequences of an issued action.
- Over what range of motion or time an acquired response remains reusable.
- Which apparently different histories can share useful behavior, and which must be distinguished for the declared goals.

The aim is not learning without assumptions. Finite data cannot determine arbitrary unobserved consequences without restrictions. The relevant comparison is the amount and kind of structure supplied, the experiences required, the computations used, and the decisions attained. A generic smoothness or finite-memory assumption is still a prior and must be disclosed; it must not be advertised as absence of prior knowledge.

The existing physical body is sufficient if needed. Continuous moving Cartesian records, ordinary command histories and fresh goal sequences could expose configuration/motion dependence without supplying joint state or a fixed acceleration formula. The exact initial position/rest and goal-change reset privileges must not return accidentally. However, choose the actual acquisition and test contract only after checking observable ambiguity, support, and feasible goals. A larger workspace is not automatically a more consequential problem.

## Serious competing explanations

**Retained structured estimation.** A conventional local response estimator may remain adequate when it retains recent motion and revises its response from ordinary feedback. If it works with less data/computation, accept that explanation. A failure of frozen constant B would not refute conventional estimation or Bayes, only that frozen family. An oracle with the actual correct physical law can diagnose information or reachability limits, but cannot be presented as an equally informed acquired method.

**Episodic conditional completion.** Whole action/observation episodes may already contain useful analogues. Conditioning retrieval on the observed prefix and using compatible continuations can reuse experience without first fitting named physical coefficients. This is a serious candidate for showing that a fitted response distribution is a practical compression rather than a fundamental requirement. Off-support interpolation, sparse matches and incompatible future actions are real failure modes, not details to conceal.

**Generic causal history learning.** A conventional flexible relation from recent observations/commands and candidate actions to later observations may acquire the relevant context dependence. The v15 lineage already makes this a serious explanation. Its Cartesian version must receive previous commands, appropriate moving-history support, and a fair planner; a deliberately pose-only or one-step-mean controller would be insufficient. Its memory, basis, optimizer and rollout assumptions remain supplied structure.

**Direct decision reuse.** A mechanism may learn useful actions conditional on history and goal without predicting all observations. This genuinely challenges prediction as a prerequisite. It needs the same sensory/interaction budget and a declared goal distribution; it cannot receive evaluator velocities, privileged rewards, counterfactual rollouts, or task-specific demonstrations that the other methods lack. It is an alternative formulation, not an instruction to train a large policy network next.

These are competing explanations, not a requirement to implement every option simultaneously. Select the smallest comparison capable of falsifying the next claim. Correct-prior Bayes remains a valid account of an optimal decision conditional on a specified joint law; the unresolved acquisition of that law or an equally useful decision relation cannot be settled by invoking optimality alone.

## Decisive observable and falsifiers

The central observable is **the total new experience needed to preserve useful decisions in a fresh moving context, given the same acquired corpus and ordinary observation opportunities**. Report physical task cost, stopping/constraint performance, interaction and computation separately. Heldout conditional consequences and uncertainty calibration diagnose *why* reuse worked or failed; they are not substitutes for task behavior. Score across independently acquired corpora, and separate early acquisition from later retained competence.

Before live comparisons, use heldout exogenous-action records with adequate overlap to test a concrete proposed interchangeability: do histories treated as equivalent show systematically different consequences under the same available action class? Avoid action collectors whose commands secretly encode unavailable motion. Observational matching without intervention support cannot establish a causal response law. Evaluator-controlled restarts or known-state twins can diagnose the ambiguity, but their extra information must not reach ordinary decisions or be counted as ordinary learning experience.

A consequential positive result would be an acquired relation supporting transfer across contexts where the constant prepared-response account no longer explains the data, with fewer supplied physical assumptions and competitive total decisions against retained conventional estimation. It need not introduce a novel algorithm. A simpler episodic or direct mechanism matching a fitted predictive mechanism could itself be a useful simplification.

Falsifiers should be fixed before outcomes:

- If a proposed distinction changes predictions but does not change useful attainable decisions, it does not establish the claimed learning need for this task family.
- If a retained ordinary estimator performs comparably, the claim that a new mechanism or richer representation is necessary fails.
- If success disappears without an oracle summary, exact preparation, test-tuned feature, or privileged counterfactual, the claimed acquisition of that missing relationship fails.
- If failures occur because tasks lack authority, data lack support, or a planner fails numerically, they do not establish representational inadequacy.
- If one-step improvements do not improve charged closed-loop decisions, the proposed practical benefit remains unsupported.

Do not create a disturbance until the preferred baseline fails. First demonstrate that the scientific ambiguity exists at feasible conditions and matters to the declared goal. If all serious mechanisms are already sufficient, identify the simpler sufficient account and redirect. The right next milestone would change what the system must acquire from experience, not merely enlarge the instrument or add another theorem about a relationship the experimenter already supplied.
