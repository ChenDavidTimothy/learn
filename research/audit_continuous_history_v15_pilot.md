# Independent audit of the continuous-history engineering pilot

**TESTED / independently verified, 2026-10-20.** This report concerns only the frozen `continuous_history_v15_pilot.py` and `continuous_history_v15_pilot.json`, not the later fresh protocol. Source/result hashes are recorded in `audit_continuous_history_v15_pilot_results.json`. Reproduce with:

```sh
.venv/bin/python research/audit_continuous_history_v15_pilot.py > research/audit_continuous_history_v15_pilot_results.json
```

The independent implementation imports no canonical research code. It reconstructs Hamiltonian dynamics, features/moments, prediction, full planned rollouts, complex-step rollout derivatives, scores and RNG. It does not rerun the source and call that independent verification.

All 72 independent exogenous-input acquisition trajectories, 4,608 plant intervals, 30 candidate fits, all validation selections, heldout one-/ten-step forecasts, ten control episodes, 1,880 control intervals and 1,800 retained action plans were checked. All 1,800 full 30-coordinate rollout gradients were independently reconstructed by batched complex-step differentiation. The maximum projected-gradient discrepancy was `3.61e-14`; fit stationarity was below `5.00e-16`. All action bounds, issued plan prefixes, shifted warm-plan costs, selected costs, solver-success flags, saturations, precision events and metrics agree. No solver failure was reported. This is a local optimizer check, not a global nonlinear control certificate; failed proposal iterates are not retained.

All position/command/noise records were reconstructed from their role-tagged `SeedSequence` paths. Seventy-four acquisition/control noise roles are distinct; paired methods intentionally reuse the same two control noise paths. The physical trajectories differ between methods. True validation diagnostics are retained but do not choose a candidate; selection uses next-observation error only. No physical/controller memory or warm plan is cleared at a goal switch. Current goal only enters planning; no future switch is disclosed.

The Fourier/context and lag feature ordering, scaling learned only from fitting data, all ridge stationarity equations and all candidate scores agree. The raw-history model selects H4; linear ARX and polynomial compression also select H4. The model named `pose` uses a current-position predictor, but its warm-started local MPC still has an indirect controller memory through the previous plan. It must not be described as a general memoryless-policy lower bound. Holding exogenous torques for two samples also correlates present and previous issued inputs at alternate steps; an omitted-history conditional model is not automatically an interventional plant model.

Hamiltonian RK4 at 32/64 substeps globally reproduces acquisition to `2.44e-12` and the ordinary low-speed control cases to `1.80e-11`. The two failed pose-predictor controllers reach speeds up to 9.37. Coarse global RK32/64 disagreement reaches `1.54e-5` there. Adaptive Hamiltonian Radau from each saved state validates local transitions to `2.61e-11`, while an independent global 18.8-second Radau replay differs by up to `8.78e-7`. The failed high-speed trajectories are locally consistent but numerically sensitive; no fine precision claim depends on their exact failed endpoints. These diagnostics are retained explicitly.

The pilot contains one acquired-data replicate and two fixed tasks; it does not support independent-seed statistical claims. Precision events among six goal windows are 0/6 for the pose predictor, 2/6 one delay, 6/6 selected raw history, 3/6 linear ARX and 6/6 polynomial compression. Their test ten-step prediction errors are respectively `0.104210`, `0.00896360`, `0.000210984`, `0.00364006`, `0.000390175`. These are an engineering observation that motivates a fresh protocol, not evidence of a new architecture or superiority over published derivative-free robotic methods. Both the supplied polynomial compressor and selected raw history work on these tasks.

The actual acquisition resources are 72 episode starts, 460.8 simulated seconds and 9,360 scalar coordinate sensor readings. Control adds ten episode starts, 188 simulated seconds and 3,780 scalar readings, including eight warmup intervals per episode. Planning records 13,888 residual calls, each with 15 learned prediction steps; fitting, forecasting, derivative checks and independent audit compute are separate. No hardware work or new general-learning mechanism is claimed. The later fresh protocol must preserve this pilot and its failures rather than present its revised settings as initially blind choices.
