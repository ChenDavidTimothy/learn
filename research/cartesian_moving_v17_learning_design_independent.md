# Independent prospective audit: learning consequences from moving Cartesian experience

2026-10-20. Read the v17 question, independent polar derivation, completed instrument audit, literature reconciliation, and verified v16 principal findings. This report selects a discriminating scientific comparison, not an implementation or a new ledger. No canonical experiment source was edited. The moving instrument supports a framing correction, not a trained learner.

## Scientific judgment

The important correction is that v16's stationary alias is exceptional. Natural motion can reveal a distinction without a specially designed probe. The correct next claim is not that every Cartesian learner needs active identification or persistent discrete modes. It is that experience can acquire how *moving observable context changes the consequences of actions*, without exact preparation, a supplied constant response law, or joint coordinates.

A successful conventional delay learner would be an acceptable answer. This partly revisits v15, and that should be acknowledged. The new scientific content would be removing v15's injective joint-coordinate interface and v16's prepared constant-response vocabulary, then showing what information in genuine camera/action experience supports useful decisions. Merely translating the v15 feature/MPC pipeline to Cartesian coordinates and reporting another success would be incremental construction. It becomes more informative if it distinguishes learned intervention consequences from passive extrapolation, and tests a simpler retained-local explanation under the same records and decisions.

No additional probe/covariance/rank theorem is required to begin that construction. Nor is a new latent architecture required. The absolute-mass counterexample already shows why passive observability of a known-body branch is a different claim from learning intervention gain.

## Simplest decisive experiment

Use one explicitly exploratory moving corpus to construct a strong conventional history learner and a serious local-estimation alternative. Freeze the data/decision/selection contract before control outcomes. Evaluate both (a) heldout action contrasts and (b) actual uninterrupted new-goal control. Acquisition alone, prediction alone, or an evaluator fork challenge alone is not the milestone.

The key additional diagnostic is deliberately small: at a prespecified collection of times on separate heldout continuous trajectories, retain the same available noisy history and let the evaluator fork the hidden physical continuation under a fixed small bank of new bounded command blocks, including zero. Each ordinary predictor sees only the history and proposed commands. Compare predicted versus actual *differences between command outcomes*, as well as absolute forecasts. These forks are evaluator interventions, not training examples, exact states, extra online probes, or a source of test-tuned actions. Count their preparation/flow cost separately.

This is more decisive than another one-step score. In a moving system, inertial drift can dominate absolute next-position accuracy. Moreover, holding random torque for two intervals makes the second command partly predictable from the previous command. A model can forecast well without correctly using the candidate action. Score fresh command-innovation transitions separately, and include heldout command blocks that vary independently of the preceding history. A same-data predictor omitting the *new proposed command* can be a diagnostic ablation, but not the main competitor. The comparison must show that the acquired relation contains useful intervention dependence, not merely good extrapolation of a correlated collector.

Do not overinterpret paired differences: with fresh independent camera noise, their noise covariance must be included. True noiseless Cartesian means may be external diagnostic scores, but must not become ordinary training targets or selection information. Keep a separate heldout measured-output score if the claim concerns predictions at the actual sensor interface.

## Minimal acquisition/support contract

The tentative broad-interior, nonzero-motion starts; eight zero intervals; then exogenous random torques held two intervals are defensible, subject to these qualifications:

- Only timestamped noisy Cartesian positions, issued torques and declared episode boundaries reach ordinary fitting. Nonzero initial motion is not supplied numerically. A zero-command interval does not certify rest.
- The eight-interval passive prelude supplies real information, time and motion. Count it. If control receives a comparable warm-up, charge it and disclose that preparation; it cannot become an unpriced source of privileged present motion.
- Split complete trajectories into fit/validation/test, and keep fork/control contexts separate. Overlapping windows from one trajectory are not independent acquired bodies. Selection uses only fit/validation information.
- Exogenous commands must be independent of hidden motion. At refresh points they must vary sufficiently to distinguish drift from action response. Full coordinate-wise command ranges alone do not certify joint history/action support.
- Broad interior *starts* do not guarantee interior trajectories. Record the actual radius, speed and action/history coverage, including passages near folded/extended poses. Do not silently discard fast or poorly predicted trajectories using evaluator state. If a restricted domain is needed, define how that restriction is available operationally and freeze it in advance.
- The training distribution should contain both weak and stronger natural motion if the scientific question includes the instrument's finite-noise boundary. Do not infer support solely from initial sampling ranges; command-held conservative motion can quickly leave them.
- Keep fixed-body generalization separate from transfer to unknown mass or actuator gain. The latter requires an actual population/intervention contract and is not established by the present moving-branch calculation.

A short acquisition pilot should report these actual coverage facts before a large control study. It must not be published as a learning advance. If support is inadequate, record that failure and revise the declared question before treating unseen control failure as evidence of a missing architecture.

## Fair serious alternatives

**Primary conventional comparator: nonlinear causal position/action-history learning with bounded predictive control.** A derivative-free GP/kernel or comparably capable regularized history model is a serious established approach, consistent with the Dalla Libera literature already inspected. It must receive past commands and enough causal context to exploit moving information; a pose-only model is not sufficient. Supply generic observation-space features rather than the true Jacobian, polar state, branch or exact acceleration formula. Do not handicap it with the short memory that happened to work for injective joint encoders in v15. Fix one rich reasonable context or a small validation-only selection before control, not a growing history menu rescued by task outcomes.

**Most useful simpler rival: retained local drift/action estimation.** A causal position filter plus a learned local sampled response can explain useful feedback without a persistent discrete world model. Let it use the same acquired corpus, timing, history and new observations; do not compare an extensively pretrained history learner only with an arbitrarily initialized estimator. The model must include moving drift and past actuation. An acceleration-affine or second-order formulation is legitimate supplied structure for this rival, but must be disclosed rather than credited as discovered. Its local validity, regularization and update schedule are chosen using training/validation, not hidden state or failed test recovery.

A frozen v16 constant-B filter can be a diagnostic reference, not the sole strong baseline. Its failure would mainly demonstrate violation of its declared approximation. If the retained local estimator performs comparably to the flexible history relation, accept that simpler explanation. If a nonlinear history relation supports better heldout action contrasts and decisions at the same experience budget, the result identifies a useful context dependence acquired from data; it does not prove a universal representation.

**Episodic conditional completion is a genuine alternative, but not automatically the next implementation.** Whole episodes can retain useful consequence relationships without fitting named physical coefficients. However, requiring nearest-neighbor matches to a long continuous future action sequence creates a severe support handicap. Failure of that particular retrieval rule would not refute memory-based learning. Before implementing it, specify a short common action-block contract with observable support, or a principled way to compose/condition episodes; otherwise it is another weak baseline. Kernel regression and episodic weighting can also be mathematically very similar, so do not manufacture an architectural distinction by renaming the same conditional estimator.

There is no need to deploy all alternatives simultaneously. The first pair should resolve whether learned context dependence buys useful decisions beyond a strong retained-local account. Novel mechanisms are warranted only if that comparison leaves a consequential uncertainty.

## Minimal decision contract

Keep the same continuous body and camera. After a declared noisy history, give only the current Cartesian target; preserve body and all ordinary memory across subsequent goals. Use goals reachable on nominal bounded trajectories at the stated sampling, not goals chosen after seeing which learner fails. Exact-state nonlinear witnesses can diagnose nominal authority externally, but do not guarantee recovery from arbitrary failed states or grant a hidden inverse to the learner.

Both methods should use the same actuator bounds, camera clock, loss and either the same planner or a clearly matched decision opportunity. Record optimizer failures, model failures and infeasible recovery separately. A finite common candidate plan bank is an option if it avoids giving one method a better optimizer, but it is not compulsory and must offer adequate authority. Future goals are not supplied early. A target defined relative to a reading must use the ordinary noisy reading, not evaluator true position disguised as a goal offset.

Charge actual task position, motion/stopping, torque, waiting and observations over the whole episode. Report instantaneous Cartesian velocity only as external evaluation; a finite-difference control penalty is a different quantity. Define whether precision is endpoint-only or sustained, and do not switch after outcomes. The acquisition and closed-loop phases must use compatible action/time support; common scalar extrema are insufficient evidence of that compatibility.

Freeze whether each method can update from new camera/action records. A retained estimator may update; a history learner may also update if that is part of the declared comparison. Any asymmetry must be explicit and interpreted as a comparison of complete mechanisms, rather than attributed solely to representation. Test targets and truth-based outcomes cannot select fitting or adaptation settings.

## Falsifiers and decision after the pilot

1. If both models accurately predict passive motion but not heldout command contrasts, the claim of acquired useful action consequences fails; more forecast accuracy does not rescue it.
2. If a retained local estimator matches the flexible relation in charged control, there is no evidence that a more elaborate learned summary is needed in this domain.
3. If the richer relation improves action contrasts but not attainable decisions, its practical control benefit remains unestablished for this contract.
4. If failures arise only outside acquired support, near singular authority limits, or from an unreliable planner, they do not establish a need for a new learning architecture.
5. If natural history already suffices, abandon the premise that a special probe or explicit branch mechanism is necessary. If older evidence is needed after motion stops, compare legitimate retained memory; do not confuse the amnesia of one finite window with universal impossibility.
6. If the only successful mechanism receives a supplied exact derivative, true branch, hidden calibration reset or test-selected feature, the promised reduction in supplied preparation/physics has not occurred.

The strongest positive result need not be novel: a conventional method can acquire a nonlinear history/action relationship from moving Cartesian experience and use it for new continuous decisions with fewer physical assumptions than v16. Its consequence is a better identified sufficient mechanism and a clear boundary, not general intelligence. If that simply reproduces v15 without distinguishing action learning from passive extrapolation or a simpler local explanation, describe it as an incremental interface extension and reconsider the next question before publishing another version.
