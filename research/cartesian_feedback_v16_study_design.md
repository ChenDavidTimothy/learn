## Scientific questions

Can useful response structure be acquired without joint state, physical branch
labels or true response matrices, then used and retained over actual new Cartesian
goals without physical/controller resets? Does prior shape matter transiently or
throughout the six-goal contract, against a full conventional Gaussian update?
Does learned directional/coupling structure add useful information beyond its mean
and total variation? The latter diagnostic is NOT a pure cross-action coherence
ablation: isotropy also changes marginal variances and the covariance spectrum.
The general grouped-covariance impossibility result does NOT establish grouping
necessity in this special arm, whose dominant variation is one radial output mode.
