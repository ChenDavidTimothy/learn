## What the pilot changes

The local mechanism's22/24 sustained precision windows show a useful conventional construction: generic causal camera/action features, same-corpus local regression and repeated feedback can work without true joint state, exact preparation or a supplied constant-B propagation law. The two failures are both first goals at one preparation; both later phases recover. Preserve their large costs rather than describe the mechanism as uniformly near the witness. The privileged computed-torque reference passes24/24, but is neither an equally informed competitor nor a global recovery certificate.

This is partly a v15 interface extension. Its defensible new content is the reduction in supplied physical information: moving, noninjective Cartesian sensing replaces joint coordinates and prepared response coefficients. A conventional sufficient construction is legitimate progress even if established methods explain it. The pilot does not derive a new learning principle or establish a universal memory/representation architecture.

Two important claims remain unsupported. First, the frozen heldout command contrasts improve only slightly over zero contrast (.9564 local MSE ratio). Accurate general intervention response has not been demonstrated across those contexts. Second, online append and offline contextual reuse are combined in the successful local controller. Neither all22successes nor recovery can yet be attributed to either component alone. ## Minimal next comparison: freeze only the acquired library

The proposed ablation is the right next experiment. Start from the EXACT selected acquired bootstrap libraries/scales. Refit the same local affine coefficients at each actual current noisy context, replan with the same optimizer, preserve ordinary histories and goal continuity, but do not append completed online rows. Pair the same eight initial/noise/warmup cases and goals. Archive the original live source/results; freeze the ablation protocol before its outcomes. Repeat only the eight necessary local episodes, not unrelated neural fitting or unchanged privileged witness generation.

Call this **frozen acquired library**, not no learning/no adaptation. Both mechanisms acquired their relation from the population corpus and can respond to new motion through query conditioning and feedback. The controlled difference is retaining newly observed causal rows for subsequent local regression. Both receive the same sensor opportunities and clocks; actual action/energy and visited contexts can diverge and must be scored.

Compare paired episode/phase costs, sustained precision and recovery, not only a pooled success count. Freeze what is reported before the new outcomes; keep every case. The closed-loop difference is the total effect of the append intervention, including its changes to future visited data. It is not an isolated estimate of improved physical gain. An optional shadow-prediction diagnostic at saved common histories can explain model changes without extra physical data, but is not required to keep adding methods before this minimal test.

Falsifiers are valuable outcomes. If frozen-library feedback matches live performance, offline conditional reuse plus ordinary feedback is sufficient here; no need for online corpus growth has been shown. If frozen is better, online rows can harm this mechanism, and that negative result should survive. If live is better, it establishes useful online retention in this bounded contract, not universal necessity or an optimal memory rule. Do not select forgetting rates or reweight observations after seeing the result merely to preserve an online-learning interpretation.

## What would justify a bounded milestone

After the ablation, freeze the complete pipeline and carry the question across independently acquired corpora and fresh control contexts/noise. Eight corpora is a reasonable continuity choice, not a statistical guarantee. Acquisition, bootstrap members and validation selection must be repeated independently; reused control cases can yield a precise conditional comparison but do not establish transfer to fresh starts. Prefer fresh prespecified control contexts with paired seeds across the two local variants. Summaries should first average within corpus, then report paired corpus differences and failure profiles. Retain early versus later goals and the actual support/authority diagnostics.

The strongest SIMPLE comparison for the immediate causal question is live versus frozen same-data local regression. It avoids an arbitrarily initialized zero-gain rival and controls the optimizer/model family. No larger neural architecture or optimizer rescue is required. The original nonlinear pipeline may remain an honestly bounded conventional comparator if replicated unchanged, but should not be the sole basis for a superiority claim. A full applicable GP/history method would matter for a later claim of superiority over modern learning approaches, not for establishing that this conventional construction suffices under the declared information contract.

A meaningful positive milestone would therefore be: a reproducible conventional data-driven local mechanism achieves useful sustained Cartesian decisions across independently acquired corpora, with the contribution or noncontribution of online experience separated from acquired contextual reuse, and with failed command-contrast generalization explicitly retained. A stable frozen result would be a useful simplification; live gains would motivate studying where new experiences remain relevant. Neither requires an invented architecture.

Do not claim that the study has identified general state-dependent physical responses accurately just because control succeeds. Moderate model accuracy can suffice through repeated feedback, while inaccurate action gain can still support robust behavior. Conversely correct fork contrasts alone would not establish useful control. The evidence can support a bounded sufficient mechanism without resolving all predictive or representational questions.

If replication is unreliable because of limited support, retain that limit and investigate which ordinary experiences distinguish the failures. If both local variants are reliably sufficient, close this instrument rather than adding another memory/optimizer menu. The consequential next uncertainty would concern transfer or revision of acquired conditional relationships beyond the sampled experience, selected on actual failures or missing assumptions rather than a harder target engineered to defeat the baseline. No ledger is warranted for the ablation alone; a verified replicated construction and coherent interpretation can warrant one. The general-learning Goal remains unresolved.
